## Data Sources

- `audit.csv`: 696 of the 700 applications in Pager (2003), <https://doi.org/10.1086/374403>. Four cases were redacted in the source copy, distributed with Imai's *Quantitative Social Science*; the course file replaces the 0/1 codes with labels.
- `largeaudit.csv` and `jobgaps.csv`: Kline, Rose, and Walters (2022), "Replication Data for: 'Systemic Discrimination Among Large U.S. Employers.'" Harvard Dataverse, V1.1, CC0 1.0. <https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/HLO4XC>. All 83,643 applications with anonymized employer and job identifiers, the firm's industry, state, submission date, the racialized-name treatment, the other randomized resume features (gender of the name, age over 40, academic, political, and LGBTQ clubs), and the contact outcome; `jobgaps.csv` is the within-job white-name minus Black-name contact difference for the 11,026 jobs that received both.
